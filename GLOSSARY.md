# Attention redirection

The app helps a person move from an activity they want to leave to an alternative they prefer.

## Language

**Nudge**:
An invitation to switch from the current activity to a chosen alternative. It leaves the decision to switch with the person.
_Avoid_: Block, punishment

**Alternative**:
An activity or destination offered in a nudge that the person would prefer to spend time on.
_Avoid_: Reward

**Monitoring session**:
A period during which the person has chosen to let the app observe their activity and offer nudges.
_Avoid_: Always-on monitoring

**Interruption rule**:
A person's plain-language instruction describing when an alternative would be welcome and when the app should stay quiet.
_Avoid_: Diagnosis, health score

**Prepared article**:
A saved reading destination accompanied by a factual brief that is ready for the app to use in an invitation.
_Avoid_: Generated article
